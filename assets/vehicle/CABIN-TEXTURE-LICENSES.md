# Cabin material sources

The photographed maps embedded in `sedan.glb` are unmodified original 1K downloads released by Poly Haven under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

- [Leather Red 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/leather_red_02), Rob Tuytel: color, OpenGL normal and ARM maps for dashboard vinyl, lower trim, pillar covering and steering surfaces.
- [Leather Red 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/leather_red_03), Rob Tuytel: color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps for recessed controls, molded borders and dark housings.
- [Jogging Melange](https://polyhaven.com/a/jogging_melange), Rico Cilliers (processing), colormass (photography): color, OpenGL normal and ARM maps for the door textile, visors and header lining.

`cabin-texture-sources.json` records every original download URL, file size and SHA-256. The shader neutralizes source color and uses fine surface detail at the authored cabin scale. The separate baked cabin AO atlas and all vehicle geometry are original authored assets.
