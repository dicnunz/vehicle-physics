# CC0 surface assets

These material maps come from Poly Haven and are released under CC0 1.0. The official license permits use and redistribution in commercial and noncommercial projects: https://polyhaven.com/license . Verified September 6, 2026.

- Asphalt 01 by Charlotte Baglioni and Dario Barresi: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_01 . Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, 2K. Source represents a 2.1 m patch.
- Aerial Grass Rock by Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock . Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, 2K. Source represents a 15 m patch.

Exact downloaded URLs and byte counts are recorded in downloads.json. No BeamNG assets are used.

- Concrete Floor 02 by Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02 . Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, 2K. CC0; 2 m patch. Used for the cast concrete proving-ground slabs.
